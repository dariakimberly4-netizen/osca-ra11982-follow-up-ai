Page 1 • Your Benefit
20%

Know what your Senior Citizen benefit means.

Under Republic Act No. 9994, qualified senior citizens may receive a 20% discount and VAT exemption, when applicable, on covered goods and services for their own use or availment.

Remember: The 20% discount does not automatically apply to every purchase. It applies only to goods and services covered by law and current implementing rules.

Page 3 • Restaurants

Eating out?

For qualified restaurant transactions, the discount applies to the portion attributable to the senior citizen's own food, drinks and covered services.

Dining with family? The entire table bill is not automatically discounted. The establishment determines the senior citizen's qualified share under applicable rules.

Good practice

  • Present your Senior Citizen ID before payment.
  • Ask for an itemized bill or receipt.
  • Check that the discount is reflected correctly.

Page 7 • Example

How the discount may be computed

For a covered VATable transaction where the displayed price already includes 12% VAT, the VAT is removed first. The 20% discount is then applied to the VAT-exempt amount.

Example only

VAT-inclusive price: ₱1,120.00

VAT-exempt base: ₱1,000.00

20% discount: ₱200.00

Estimated amount to pay: ₱800.00

Important: Actual computation can depend on the type of transaction and current regulations.

Page 8 • Quick Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can I use my discount when paying by credit card?

Qualified purchases remain subject to the Senior Citizen benefit even when payment is made by credit card, subject to current rules.

What if an item is already on promotion?

Where applicable, the senior citizen generally avails of the more favorable discount under the current rules.

Can I combine Senior Citizen and PWD discounts?

The two statutory discounts are not stacked on the same transaction.

Can I use my OSCA ID outside my city?

A valid original OSCA-issued Senior Citizen ID is generally honored nationwide under the implementing rules.

Where can the 20% discount apply?

RA 9994 covers specific goods and services. The transaction must be for the senior citizen’s own use or availment.

💊

Medicines & Medical Supplies

Qualified medicines and prescribed medical supplies, accessories and equipment covered by applicable rules.

🏥

Private Medical & Dental Services

Covered medical and dental services, diagnostic and laboratory tests, and qualified professional fees in private facilities.

🍽️

Restaurants

Qualified food, drinks and services for the senior citizen’s exclusive consumption or availment, subject to the implementing rules.

🚌

Domestic Transportation

Qualified fares for domestic land, air and sea transportation under applicable regulations.

🏨

Hotels & Lodging

Qualified room and lodging services in covered hotels and similar lodging establishments.

🎟️

Recreation & Admission

Qualified fees for covered recreation centers, theaters, cinemas, concert halls, museums, parks and similar places.

⚱️

Funeral & Burial Services

Qualified funeral and burial services for a deceased senior citizen, subject to the requirements in the implementing rules.

💳

Credit Card Payments

The 20% discount and VAT exemption may still apply to qualified purchases even when payment is made by credit card.

How to claim your discount

Present proof of eligibility.
Show your valid original OSCA-issued Senior Citizen ID or other proof accepted under current rules.
Tell the establishment before payment.
Ask to avail of the Senior Citizen discount for the qualified goods or services.
Check the computation.
Confirm that the applicable VAT exemption and 20% discount were correctly reflected on the bill or invoice.
Keep your receipt.
Keep the official receipt or invoice in case you need to verify the computation or raise a concern.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is every purchase automatically entitled to 20% off?

No. The law lists covered goods and services, and the benefit is generally for the senior citizen’s exclusive use, enjoyment or availment.

Is the 20% discount computed before or after VAT?

For a covered VATable transaction, the senior citizen is VAT-exempt. The VAT is removed from the VAT-inclusive selling price first, and the 20% discount is applied to the VAT-exempt amount.

Can I still receive the discount when paying by credit card?

Yes, for qualified purchases. The implementing rules state that the 20% discount and VAT exemption also apply to purchases paid through credit cards.

What if an item is already on promotion?

For promotional discounts covered by the implementing rules, the senior citizen may generally avail of either the promotional discount or the statutory senior citizen discount, whichever is higher and more favorable, subject to applicable rules.

Can I combine Senior Citizen and PWD discounts?

The two statutory discounts should not be stacked for the same transaction. A person who qualifies under both should avail of the applicable discount allowed for that transaction.

Does the Senior Citizen ID work outside my city?

The RA 9994 implementing rules provide that a valid original OSCA-issued Senior Citizen ID shall be honored nationwide.

Know your rights

If you believe a qualified discount was incorrectly denied: politely ask the establishment to review the Senior Citizen discount rules and request an itemized receipt or invoice. You may then bring the concern and supporting documents to the appropriate OSCA or government office for guidance.

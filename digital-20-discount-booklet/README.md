# Digital Senior Citizen Booklet — 20% Discount Guide

A mobile-first informational website focused only on the Philippine Senior Citizen 20% discount and VAT exemption for qualified goods and services under Republic Act No. 9994 and its Implementing Rules and Regulations.

## Features
- Large senior-friendly typography and touch targets
- Covered goods and services overview
- Simple step-by-step claiming guide
- FAQ section
- Quick calculator for covered VATable transactions where the entered amount is VAT-inclusive
- Responsive single-page design

## Legal note
This site is an informational guide and does not replace Republic Act No. 9994, its Implementing Rules and Regulations, BIR regulations, agency circulars, or other official government guidance.
